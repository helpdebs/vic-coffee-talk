Teacher notes

Follow-up questions

  • Did any of your sources disagree with each other?
  • Which source did you trust the most? Why?
  • Was it easier to find benefits or risks?
  • Which fact would you tell a friend at the café?

Listen for

  • Reporting verbs: suggest, claim, show, point out
  • Hedging: seems to, may, tends to
  • Numbers said naturally: about four cups, up to 400 milligrams

Teacher notes

Push for a reason

  • Why do you think so?
  • Did you read anything about that?
  • Who usually says that? Your parents? Social media?

After a wrong guess

  • So what's the real story?
  • Ask her to correct the statement in her own words: "Actually, decaf still has…"
